🌏 Family Traditions in Different Cultures

Every family has its own traditions. A custom is something that people do because it is traditional. Traditions help families to stay together and to show respect to each other.

In many countries, people shake hands when they meet. But in Japan, people often bow. In some countries, people exchange gifts at Christmas. In other countries, families celebrate with a big meal.

Manners are also important. It is polite to say "please" and "thank you". It is impolite to talk with your mouth full. When a guest comes to your house, you should greet them and offer them tea or coffee. In many Asian homes, you should remove your shoes before entering.

A gesture can also be a greeting. For example, a smile is a friendly gesture. But some gestures mean different things in different cultures. So when you travel, it is important to follow local customs and to be polite.
